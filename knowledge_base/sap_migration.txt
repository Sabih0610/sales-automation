Royal Cyber SAP — Migration & Integration with Microsoft Fabric

WHAT IT IS:
Royal Cyber helps enterprises migrate SAP workloads to the cloud
and integrate SAP data with Microsoft Fabric for unified analytics
across ERP and business intelligence.

PROBLEMS IT SOLVES:
- SAP on-premise is expensive to maintain and difficult to scale.
- SAP data sits isolated from the rest of the business data.
- Reporting on SAP data requires expensive SAP BW or third-party tools.
- SAP upgrades (S/4HANA migration) are complex and high-risk.

HOW ROYAL CYBER HELPS:
We handle SAP to Azure migration, connect SAP with Fabric via
certified connectors, and build unified dashboards combining SAP
data with other business data sources.

TYPICAL OUTCOMES:
- 35-45% reduction in SAP infrastructure costs post-migration
- SAP data available in Fabric within hours not days
- Unified reporting across SAP, CRM, and other systems
- Reduced dependency on expensive SAP-specific BI tools

TARGET PERSONAS:
- CTO / CIO: SAP modernisation and cost reduction
- SAP Architects: technical migration planning
- CFO: infrastructure cost reduction and ROI
