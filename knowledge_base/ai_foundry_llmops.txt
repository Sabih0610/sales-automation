Royal Cyber Azure AI Foundry — LLM Operations

WHAT IT IS:
Azure AI Foundry (formerly Azure AI Studio) is Microsoft's platform
for building, deploying, and managing enterprise AI applications
using large language models including GPT-4, Phi-3, and custom models.

PROBLEMS IT SOLVES:
- Companies want to use AI but lack the infrastructure to deploy
  and manage LLMs securely at enterprise scale.
- Proof-of-concept AI projects never reach production.
- Data privacy concerns prevent use of public AI APIs.
- AI costs spiral without proper usage management.

HOW ROYAL CYBER HELPS:
We implement AI Foundry end-to-end — from model selection and
fine-tuning to production deployment with security, monitoring,
and cost controls. We build on your existing Azure investment.

TYPICAL OUTCOMES:
- Production AI applications deployed in 6-10 weeks
- Enterprise data never leaves your Azure tenant
- 40% cost reduction vs public API usage at scale
- Full audit trails for AI outputs (compliance ready)
