Royal Cyber Microsoft Fabric — Lakehouse Architecture

WHAT IT IS:
Microsoft Fabric Lakehouse unifies data lakes and data warehouses
into a single platform. It eliminates the need to maintain separate
systems for raw data storage and analytical processing.

PROBLEMS IT SOLVES:
- Companies managing separate data lakes (Azure Data Lake, S3) and
  data warehouses (Synapse, Snowflake, Redshift) face high costs,
  complex ETL pipelines, and data consistency issues.
- Data engineering teams spend 60-70% of time on data movement
  instead of analytics.
- Business teams wait days for reports because data pipelines are
  slow and fragile.

HOW ROYAL CYBER HELPS:
Royal Cyber implements Fabric Lakehouse from assessment to production
in 8-12 weeks. We migrate existing data infrastructure, rebuild
pipelines natively in Fabric, and train internal teams.

TYPICAL OUTCOMES:
- 40-60% reduction in data infrastructure costs
- Pipeline development time cut from weeks to hours
- Single source of truth across all business units
- Real-time dashboards replacing weekly reports

TARGET PERSONAS:
- CTO / CIO: infrastructure cost reduction, tech debt elimination
- Head of Data: unified data platform, faster pipeline development
- VP Analytics: self-service analytics, real-time reporting

RELEVANT INDUSTRIES:
Retail, Manufacturing, Financial Services, Healthcare, Logistics
